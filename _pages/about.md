I am a junior lecturer and PhD Student in Linguistics at the University of Bayreuth. I am co-supervised by [Prof. Dr. Markus Bieswanger](https://www.english-linguistics2.uni-bayreuth.de/de/team/bieswanger/index.php) and [Prof. Dr. Ole Schützler](https://www.uni-leipzig.de/personenprofil/mitarbeiter/prof-dr-ole-schuetzler). 

My main interests include speech and language processing, computational linguistics, ASR, and all things phonetics. My research background is in Sociophonetics and Phonetics, but lately I am transitioning into computational linguistics. In my PhD I conduct a large corpus analysis of several corpora investigating the use of various grammatical features in Scottish English. I do so combining various traditional corpus and grammar research approaches with custom trained and built AI models.

I am also doing some work on ASR models in the aviation space. Check out the programme of the recent [IALCAER](https://www.ialcaer.uni-bayreuth.de/en/index.html) conference I co-organised, if you want to know more.

Prior to my PhD studies, I completed a MSc and MScR in Linguistics at the University of Edinburgh, both with a focus on NLP and Phonetics, and BA studies in English/Politics/History at the Otto-Friedrich University Bamberg.
